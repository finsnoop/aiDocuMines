# aiDocuMines — Platform Voiceover Script

> **Target Audience:** Managing Partners, Senior Associates, Operations Leads, and Technology Decision Makers at law firms, private equity firms, and corporate legal departments.

> **Tone:** Confident, professional, slightly conversational. Not salesy — authoritative.

> **Estimated Runtime:** 6–8 minutes

> **Note:** Services, tools, and integrations are continuously expanding. This script reflects the current feature set and will be updated as new capabilities are added.

## SCENE 3 — Dashboard Overview

**[Visual: Dashboard home with customizable widget grid — KPIs, analytics charts, activity feeds, recent files]**

**Voiceover:**

When you log in, the dashboard gives you an immediate overview of your workspace. The home page is fully customizable — drag, drop, and configure widgets to surface the metrics that matter to you. Processing KPIs, file volumes by type, recent activity, storage utilization, security status, compliance alerts — all visible at a glance.

The left sidebar gives you access to your full file library. My Files, Shared Files, archived documents — everything is organized by matter and client. You're never more than two clicks away from any document in the system.

Up top, you have Quick Find for instant search, an Activity Log so you always know who accessed what and when, and direct access to Support.

## SCENE 4 — File Management & Upload

**[Visual: Navigate to Uploads page, drag-and-drop files, show upload progress]**

**Voiceover:**

Uploading documents is simple. Drag and drop entire folders — hundreds of files at once. aiDocuMines supports over twenty-seven file formats out of the box. PDFs, Word documents, Excel spreadsheets, PowerPoint presentations, images, even scanned documents.

Each file is automatically indexed, OCR-processed for scanned content, and made searchable the moment it lands in the system. No manual tagging required.

## SCENE 6 — AI Document Processing

**[Visual: Select AI Document Processing vertical, show the seven core services: Semantic Search, Document Clustering, Text Search, Document Q&A, Anonymization, OCR, Structure Analysis]**

**Voiceover:**

The AI Document Processing vertical provides the core intelligence layer. AI Semantic Search uses vector embeddings to find conceptually related documents — not just keyword matches. Document Clustering automatically groups files by content similarity. AI Document Q&A lets you ask natural-language questions and get precise answers drawn from your documents.

There's also PII Anonymization to strip sensitive information, OCR Text Extraction for scanned documents, and Document Structure Analysis to map out sections, headings, and hierarchies automatically.

---

## SCENE 7 — Single & Bulk Processing

**[Visual: Select a document from the sidebar, click a service, show processing progress, view results in right panel. Then select a folder to show bulk mode.]**

**Voiceover:**

Processing a document is straightforward. Select the file from your file library, click the service, and aiDocuMines gets to work. Results appear in the right panel, side by side with the original.

And this works at scale. Select an entire folder, and the platform automatically switches to bulk mode. Run any service across every document in that folder simultaneously. A hundred contracts, a thousand pages — processed in parallel.

You can monitor progress in real time with the Jobs tab, and when it's done, review outputs for every file in one place. This is what turns a two-week review into an afternoon.

## SCENE 8 — DocuChat (AI Document Chat)

**[Visual: Navigate to Chat page, show conversation sidebar, ask a question about a document]**

**Voiceover:**

Sometimes you don't need a full extraction. You just need an answer.

DocuChat lets you have a natural language conversation with your documents. Ask it: "What are the indemnification limits in this contract?" or "Summarize the key risk factors in the Q filing."

The AI reads the document, understands the context, and gives you a precise answer. You can start new topics, generate summaries of your conversations, or download individual responses for your records.

## SCENE 9 — Workbench (Side-by-Side Comparison)

**[Visual: Navigate to Editor/Workbench, show two document panels side by side, toggle view modes]**

**Voiceover:**

The Workbench is built for document comparison and review. Load an original contract on the left, and a redlined version on the right. Toggle between side-by-side, stacked, original-only, and edited-only views with a single click.

For Office documents, aiDocuMines renders Word, Excel, and PowerPoint files directly in the browser using OnlyOffice integration — no downloads, no switching applications. Everything stays in one view.

## SCENE 10 — Private Equity Solutions

**[Visual: Navigate to Services, select Private Equity category, show the 15 AI Solutions and 19 Deal Operations sub-categories]**

**Voiceover:**

For private equity teams, aiDocuMines goes deeper. Thirty-four dedicated services cover the entire deal lifecycle.

**[Visual: Show AI Solutions cards — Deal Sourcing, IC Copilot, Commercial DD, QoE Analyzer, Contract Intelligence, LBO Modeler, Early Warning, Exit Readiness]**

Fifteen AI-powered solutions handle the analytical heavy lifting: Deal-Sourcing Intelligence scores inbound deals against your fund thesis. The IC Copilot generates draft investment memos with risk flags and comparable comps. Quality-of-Earnings normalizes EBITDA and validates reported financial performance. The LBO Valuation Copilot builds models, sensitivity analyses, and IRR projections. Exit-Readiness Platform handles buyer-matching and data room preparation.

**[Visual: Switch to Deal Operations sub-category — Workspace, Data Room Sync, Issue Spotting, Clause Variance, Closing Checklist, CP Tracker, Closing Binder]**

Nineteen deal operations tools cover the mechanics: creating deal workspaces, syncing data rooms, AI issue spotting across documents, clause variance analysis against your playbooks, closing checklists, condition precedent trackers, and closing binder generation.

Each solution is built on DSPy — our AI reasoning engine — that uses whatever language model your team configures. No vendor lock-in. No hardcoded models. As AI models improve, your platform improves automatically.

## SCENE 11 — Legal Verticals

**[Visual: Quick montage cycling through Class Actions, Labor Employment, IP Litigation, Regulatory Compliance services]**

Beyond private equity, aiDocuMines serves four additional legal verticals.

Each vertical is built around the specific workflows legal teams actually use — not generic AI tools forced into legal contexts.
